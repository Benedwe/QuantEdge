## Full description

QuantEdge Forex Analyst is a companion skill for the QuantEdge AI forex
dashboard. Explore the [hosted demo](https://ai-powered-forex-tra-byyt.bolt.host/)
or inspect the [open-source project](https://github.com/Benedwe/QuantEdge), then
use the skill to understand the app, review forex data you provide, compare
conditional bullish and bearish scenarios, estimate position size from your own
account balance and risk budget, or structure a trade-journal review.

The workflow separates supplied facts from calculations and interpretation,
checks data timestamps, and identifies missing inputs rather than inventing
market prices or economic events. An included calculator estimates standard FX
lot size and rounds down to the selected lot increment.

The skill does not fetch prices, connect to broker accounts, or execute trades.
The dashboard is a separate demo that can show generated/simulated market data
and includes optional broker integration; verify data sources and account
permissions independently. Neither the demo nor this skill guarantees results.
Position-size estimates exclude trading costs and execution effects unless
specifically provided.
